# Quotation Evidence Workspace

This folder will contain real Deenar Lift Company quotations provided for business verification.

---

## 1. Purpose

- This folder will contain real Deenar Lift Company quotations provided for business verification.
- Quotations are evidence sources, not automatically public website claims.

---

## 2. Future Workflow

```
QUOTATIONS
→ EVIDENCE EXTRACTION
→ BUSINESS OWNER VERIFICATION
→ APPROVED BUSINESS TRUTH
→ WEBSITE CLAIMS
```

---

## 3. Rules

- Do not invent or infer information.
- A quotation showing something once does not automatically mean it is a general company capability.
- Technical specifications must remain traceable to the original quotation.
- Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, emails, quotation numbers, and other unnecessary personal/customer information should be redacted before analysis.
- Do not publish quotation information directly on the website without business-owner verification.
- Pricing from quotations must not be treated as current public pricing without verification.
- Multiple quotations may reveal patterns, but patterns still require business-owner confirmation.

---

## 4. Suggested File Naming

Use a consistent format such as:

```
quotation-001.pdf
quotation-002.pdf
quotation-003.pdf
```

Do not rename files in a way that changes or invents information about the quotation.
